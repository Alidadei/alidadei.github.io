山峦册山

山峰信息收集册 · Available · 2026

A collection album of Chinese mountains · 2026

A collection album of 45 famous Chinese mountains — with real photos, weather-aware picks and distance. As a bonus, the stairs you climb every day count too: a virtual climb up real trails.

一本收录 45 座中国名山的信息收集册——实景相册、看天推荐、离你多远,一页一档案。附带的小心思:你每天爬的楼梯也算数,化作一场沿真实山径的虚拟登山。

🚀 Try it live · 📦 GitHub 🚀 在线体验 · 📦 GitHub 仓库

Why stairs? 🧗

为什么是楼梯?🧗

Most of us can't climb a real mountain every week — but stairs are everywhere. One floor ≈ 3 meters of elevation. Save it up, and one day you "summit" a famous peak.

多数人没法每周去爬山,但楼梯到处都有。一层楼 ≈ 3 米爬升——把每天的爬楼攒起来,有一天你就「登顶」了一座名山。

The climb feels real 📍

过程像真的在爬山 📍

You advance along the actual trail of the mountain — real landmarks light up one by one, the app predicts your summit date, and at the top you get a certificate with a red seal.

你沿着这座山真实的登山路线前进——沿途真实地标逐一点亮,应用预测你的登顶日期,登顶后获得一张盖红章的中式证书。

Six peaks to conquer 🎯

六座挑战山峰 🎯

泰山 1,545m→ 华山 2,154m→ 峨眉山 3,079m→ … → 玉龙雪山 5,596m→ 珠峰 8,848m

From a weekend hill to Everest — the ladder keeps going. Plus: a 45-mountain album with real photo galleries, an AI buddy (voice in, voice out) that explains its picks, weather-aware recommendations computed by GitHub Actions, GPX import, a stamp-collection passport and 10 achievement badges. Installable as a PWA — it works offline in the mountains.

从一座周末小山到珠穆朗玛——梯子一直向上。此外还有:45 座名山档案与实景相册、会解释推荐理由的 AI 搭子「山灵」(支持语音对话)、跑在 GitHub Actions 上的看天推荐、GPX 轨迹一键导入、山峰护照集章和 10 枚成就徽章。可安装为 PWA——山里没信号也能用。

A quiet piece of engineering 🛠

一点安静的小工程 🛠

No install, no account, no backend. Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS on GitHub Pages; your data never leaves the browser. The cover is a public-domain scan of Wang Ximeng's "A Thousand Li of Rivers and Mountains" (1113 AD); mountain photos come from Wikimedia Commons with credits. A recommendation engine runs daily on GitHub Actions — it reads the weather forecast and writes a static JSON, so even the recommender is "hosted" by git.

不用安装、不用注册、没有后端——纯 HTML/CSS/JS 跑在 GitHub Pages 上,数据永远不离开浏览器。主页横幅是王希孟《千里江山图》(北宋,公有领域)的高清扫描,名山实拍来自 Wikimedia Commons 并逐张记录版权;推荐引擎每天在 GitHub Actions 上读天气预报、算好榜单写成静态 JSON——连推荐系统都是 git 托管的。
