i爬山 ⛰️

A hiking check-in app with a twist: the stairs you climb every day also count — as a virtual climb up real mountains.

一个记录爬山的网页应用,还有一个小心思:你每天爬的楼梯,也能算数——化作一场沿着真实山径的虚拟登山。

🚀 Try it live · 📦 GitHub 🚀 在线体验 · 📦 GitHub 仓库

Why stairs? 🧗

为什么是楼梯?🧗

Most of us can't climb a real mountain every week — but stairs are everywhere. One floor ≈ 3 meters of elevation. Save it up, and one day you "summit" a famous peak.

多数人没法每周去爬山,但楼梯到处都有。一层楼 ≈ 3 米爬升——把每天的爬楼攒起来,有一天你就「登顶」了一座名山。

The climb feels real 📍

过程像真的在爬山 📍

You advance along the actual trail of the mountain — real landmarks light up one by one, the app predicts your summit date, and at the top you get a certificate with a red seal.

你沿着这座山真实的登山路线前进——沿途真实地标逐一点亮,应用预测你的登顶日期,登顶后获得一张盖红章的中式证书。

Six peaks to conquer 🎯

六座挑战山峰 🎯

泰山 1,545m→ 华山 2,154m→ 峨眉山 3,099m→ … → 贡嘎 7,556m→ 珠峰 8,848m

From a weekend hill to Everest — the ladder keeps going. Plus: a guidebook of 15 famous mountains, an AI buddy that recommends "a mountain for you this weekend", GPX track import, stamp collecting and 8 achievement badges.

从一座周末小山到珠穆朗玛——梯子一直向上。此外还有:15 座名山图鉴、会推荐「这周末适合爬哪座山」的 AI 搭子、GPX 轨迹一键导入、山峰护照集章和 8 枚成就徽章。

A quiet piece of engineering 🛠

一点安静的小工程 🛠

No install, no account, no server. It's one HTML file of vanilla JavaScript — every mountain landscape and elevation profile you see is drawn by code (seeded SVG), not a photo. Your data never leaves your phone's browser.

不用安装、不用注册、没有服务器——它就是一个纯 JavaScript 网页。你看到的所有山景和海拔剖面图都是代码画出来的(种子化 SVG),不是照片;你的数据也永远不离开手机浏览器。
